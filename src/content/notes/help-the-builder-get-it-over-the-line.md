Someone with an idea can now build an interface, connect a database, add payments and put a working product in front of people. That is a real achievement. The fact that an experienced engineer was not needed for every early step is part of what makes this moment so interesting.

The difficulty arrives when the visible product is ahead of the foundations underneath it. A bug exposes another bug. An edge case changes the workflow. Questions appear around accessibility, security, recovery and what happens when real users do something the prompt never anticipated.

That is where I see my work fitting. I would rather help the builder understand what they have, protect what is already good, and carry it into a state they can confidently put in front of customers.

People often call this the final 10 or 20 percent. It may not be 10 percent of the engineering effort, but it is the distance between something that works in a demo and something ready to be trusted.

Vibe coding opens the door to far more builders. The next opportunity is helping more of their ideas make it safely through the door.
