## Evidence and method

The 293-post analytics export captured 14 August 2026 supplied the historic comparison. Later published Source posts and the 20 October closing intake supplied newer candidates. Individual lifetime analytics for all ten below were refreshed directly on LinkedIn on 20 October, and all ten original images were recovered from their matching live posts. The shortlist combines site relevance, firsthand substance, useful reader response, freshness and overlap; it is not an exhaustive all-time numerical top ten. Article announcements, shares of others’ work and near-duplicates were deprioritised.

Engagement rate = social engagements ÷ impressions × 100. Social engagements sum reactions, comments, reposts, saves and sends. All ten component totals reconcile. Comments may include author replies; impressions are not unique readers. Older and newer posts have different accumulation time; small samples make rates volatile. Follower attribution does not establish buyer intent or sales.
