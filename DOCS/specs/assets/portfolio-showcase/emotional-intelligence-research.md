## Book evidence, read scope and interpretation

Reviewed author-published excerpts of Search Inside Yourself, not the entire book: https://siybook.com/excerpts/ . Author Chade-Meng Tan reports six distinguishing technology-worker competencies, four emotional and two intellectual. He describes mindfulness-related practices and emotional skills as trainable. The private page now summarises the six/four finding with attribution rather than listing all six, following James’s request for gentler, simpler prose. This is Tan’s account of cited research; original six-factor study was not independently identified or reanalysed. Do not call it a new Google experiment or universal causal finding, and do not say IQ is irrelevant. The recalled five/three figure was corrected from primary text.

Author’s2012Wharton interview explicitly discusses engineers and repeats six/four: https://knowledge.wharton.upenn.edu/podcast/knowledge-at-wharton-podcast/googles-chade-meng-tan-wants-you-to-search-inside-yourself-for-inner-and-world-peace/ . It explains attention training, awareness and habits; anecdotal programme results are not randomised evidence. Useful accessible companion to the excerpt.

## Goleman’s workplace framework

Read the author’s book chapter An EI-Based Theory of Performance, from The Emotionally Intelligent Workplace, reprinted with permission: https://www.eiconsortium.org/reprints/ei_theory_performance.html . Goleman distinguishes underlying EI capacity from learned workplace competence. His older framework has five domains, later revised to four; these frameworks are separate from Tan’s six performance competencies. Do not conflate them. Clear basis for the page wording that emotional competencies are learned capabilities alongside technical skills.

Supporting training report examined: https://www.eiconsortium.org/reports/technical_report.html . Use as development guidance, not validation of James’s programme or a short session’s outcomes.

## Independent programme study and limits

Caporale-Berkowitz and colleagues,2021, Search inside yourself: investigating the effects of a widely adopted mindfulness-at-work development program. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10817720/ .123employees across3offices, self-report pre/post/fourweekfollow-up;96postcourse,71follow-up. Found greater reported mindfulness and emotional awareness at follow-up, but no significant follow-up improvements in emotional management, active listening or burnout. No control group and attrition; no objective job-performance measurement. Supports cautious interest in awareness training, not a promise of better engineering output or proof meditation caused the changes. Later evidence, not something James could have read at JUMO2014–16.

## Copy decisions

Foreground the authentic engineering-training origin and practical awareness examples. Attribute six/four to Tan, link the book excerpt, and cite Goleman for learned competencies. Avoid binary amygdala-versus-prefrontal explanations, EQ-trumps-IQ absolutes, medical claims and invented programme outcomes. Keep the detailed research limitations in this editorial evidence note; the service pages make bounded experiential claims and contain no claim that James’s course was scientifically evaluated or affiliated with Google/SIYLI.
