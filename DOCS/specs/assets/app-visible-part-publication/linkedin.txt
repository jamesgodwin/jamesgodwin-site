A friend once told me he wanted to vibe-code the next Airbnb.

He’d watched a tutorial with listings, a booking screen, a map and a working checkout. You really can build that part quickly now.

What I kept thinking about was everything the screens don’t show. Who holds the money? What happens when a payment is disputed after the host has been paid? Who manages the listings, handles a refund or helps when a stay goes wrong?

The demo is a useful beginning. The question I find interesting comes next: what does this product need before a stranger can trust it?
