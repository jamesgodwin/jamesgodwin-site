My Mac was reporting 348 GB used, and I couldn’t explain where it had all gone.

I gave Codex one instruction: investigate, but don’t delete or modify anything.

It found temporary caches, old development tools, a local virtual machine and Docker storage. Some could be regenerated. Others needed a closer look before I touched them.

I eventually removed about 81 GB. The useful part was understanding what was there before deciding what could go.
